Today was the first go at carving away at college heights.

Modern small city suburbia is the 10th level of torment. Each new piece of sprawl in PG comes strapped with at minimum one pre-built basement suite ready and hot to go for you to rent out at a significant portion of your mortgage. Is your home truly yours alone in the cold suburbs if you cannot fuck or fight with your wife in the living room with earwigs in the walls? When I see new builds with three suites in the basement, I ask - when do you stop being a home owner and start becoming an apartment manager?

I would prefer to believe it's purely aesthetic, but I know it's not. You got your big job, your big raise. It's time to get the home you and the wife want outside of the so-called city. But we know that's not really affordable on a PG salary. The cheques aren't that big. So it can't be about aesthetic if you're willing to have the secondary garage for the rental sitting right next to yours on display. What people are buying is the aesthetic of power. Power doesn't quite belong to them, no one actually working for a living has that, but you can mortage a piece of it. It just costs you a basement and a soul.
